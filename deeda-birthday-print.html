Chapter V — The Letter

Something I’ve Been Meaning to Say

There are things that live between conversations. Things I wrote in my head a hundred times and never sent. Until now.

Deeda,

There are people you meet and you know — the way you always know, in fiction — that they matter.

You were one of those people. You still are. You always will be.

The thing about being friends with you is that it doesn’t feel like something I chose. It feels like something that was already written — somewhere in the margins of a book, between the lines of a scene we both watched at the same time in different places.

You’re the person I send the voice note to at 3AM. The one who gets why the fictional grief is actually real grief. The one who doesn’t need it explained.

You, me, and Hafsu — we’re the kind of trio that doesn’t happen twice. But you and I specifically have something that lives in the pages and the frames. In the stories that broke us open.

I’ve thought about how to say this properly for a long time.

I don’t think there’s a proper way. There’s only this: thank you for being the kind of person who stays. Who spirals with me. Who grieves fictional characters like they deserve to be grieved.

Happy 19th, Deeda. May this year be as full of feeling as every story we ever loved — and may you always, always know you are the best part of mine.

— always, your person
Thank you for existing.
May 19 be the year every story you love starts feeling like it was written for you — because it was.
